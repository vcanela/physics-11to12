Year 11 Physics Catch-Up

The Year 11 ideas you need before starting Level 2 Physics, worked through at your own pace.

How to use these pages
  1. Work through the topics in order. The maths toolkit comes first because everything else uses it.
  2. Read the key ideas and worked examples, then do the questions in your book with full working.
  3. Calculation questions let you check your final number. The number is the least important part: what matters is how you got there, so write every step.
  4. Explain questions have no answers here. Write your best attempt in your book, and come back to it later to see if you can improve it.
  5. Anything that confuses you is useful. Write it down so you can come back to it.

Topics

  1. 1

    Maths toolkit

    Units, scientific notation, significant figures, rearranging formulae, reading graphs, and right-angle trigonometry. Every Level 2 calculation depends on these.

  2. 2

    Mechanics

    Speed, acceleration, motion graphs, forces, energy, work and power. Leads into Level 2 vectors, projectiles, momentum and circular motion.

  3. 3

    Electricity and magnetism

    Circuits, current, voltage, resistance, power and magnetic fields. Leads into Level 2 electric fields, DC circuits and electromagnetism.

  4. 4

    Waves and light

    Wave properties, the wave equation, reflection and refraction. Leads into Level 2 mirrors, lenses and wave behaviour.

  5. 5

    Writing explanations

    How to answer “explain” and “discuss” questions. This is what moves an answer from Achieved to Merit and Excellence.

  6. 6

    Graphs and investigations

    Plotting data, lines of best fit, and turning curves into straight lines. Prepares you for the Level 2 practical investigation.

Self-paced practice. There is no marking on these pages: your working is what counts.
